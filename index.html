NEED TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know before booking ContentByZanaira.

What types of events do you cover? +

I specialise in weddings, nikkahs, parties, celebrations and business events. I capture the special moments, details and behind-the-scenes content that you’ll want to look back on and share.

What does a content creator do at an event? +

I capture natural, candid and engaging moments throughout your event, including behind-the-scenes clips, details, guest interactions and key moments. Depending on your package, you can receive professionally edited videos and/or raw footage.

What equipment do you use? +

I use a DJI Osmo Pocket 3 and iPhone to capture high-quality, smooth and natural footage that is perfect for Instagram, TikTok and other social media platforms.

Do you travel outside Bedfordshire? +

Yes! I’m happy to travel outside Bedfordshire. A travel fee of £0.75 per mile applies for bookings outside Bedfordshire.

How far in advance should I book? +

I recommend booking as early as possible, particularly for weddings and popular dates, as availability can fill up quickly. Your date is only secured once the required deposit has been paid.

Do you provide raw footage? +

Yes! Raw footage is available depending on the package you choose. If you’d like all your clips to keep and use yourself, just let me know when booking.

How quickly will I receive my edited content? +

Edited content is typically delivered within 24–36 hours after your event, depending on the package and amount of content required.

Can I request specific content or videos? +

Absolutely! I encourage you to send over reference videos, ideas, trends or specific moments you’d like captured. I’ll do my best to bring your vision to life while also capturing natural moments throughout the event.

Do you require a deposit? +

Yes. A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking and date. The remaining balance will be due according to the booking terms.

Do you create content for businesses? +

Yes! I create content for businesses, brands and local businesses, including promotional videos, social media content, product/service videos and behind-the-scenes content to help you grow your online presence.

Can I book you for both my wedding and another event? +

Of course! If you have multiple events coming up, feel free to get in touch and I can discuss your requirements and create a bespoke package to suit you.

How do I book? +

Simply get in touch with your event date, location, timings and the package you’re interested in. I’ll check my availability and provide you with the relevant pricing and booking information.
